How we use your information

These pages are made by the Manotsava 2026 EEG team, NIMHANS, Bengaluru, to explain how EEG works. We collect a small amount of information to learn who the pages reach and how they are used.

The visitor form

Before the pages open we ask a few questions through a Google Form: which group describes you (for example student, clinician or member of the public), your age group, gender, where you are visiting from, how you heard about the pages, and whether you have seen an EEG before. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number. Answers are stored in Google Forms and Google Sheets and are seen only by the EEG team.

Usage measurement

After you submit the form, the pages load two measurement tools:

  • Google Analytics 4 records which pages you open, how long they are in view, how far you scroll, which buttons and links you use, your device and browser type, and your approximate location (city or region, worked out from your internet address).
  • Microsoft Clarity records clicks, scrolling and pointer movement so we can see which parts of the interactive pages are confusing, as heatmaps and session replays. Clarity hides text you type.

Both tools set cookies or similar storage in your browser. The pages also remember in your browser that you have filled the form, so you are not asked again. You can remove all of this by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

What we do not do

We do not sell this information, use it for advertising, or try to identify individual visitors. We report it only as totals, such as how many students visited or how long people spent on each page.
